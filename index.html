Go2 Museum Digital Twin

A walkable 3D reconstruction of a museum gallery, built from a Unitree Go2 quadruped's LiDAR, IMU and RGB-D camera. The robot's LiDAR gives the geometry, its trajectory is corrected with loop closure, and the camera photos supply the textures.

What you are looking at

  • A single-floor gallery, about 26 × 18 m, scanned during one 7-minute, 120 m walk of the robot.
  • Walls are shown up to about 2.1 m. Nothing higher was observed (camera at 0.55 m, LiDAR facing down), so no ceiling or upper walls are invented.
  • Lighting is the real gallery lighting captured in the photos; a soft key light adds relief.
  • The green-to-orange line (T) is the robot's reconstructed path.
